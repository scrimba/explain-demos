    What is 360DMax ?

    A productive employee is an asset to the organization, and knowing the productivity of an employee is a challenging task to perform. But no more, it is so. You can measure the usefulness of an employee by laying your hands on a wealth of data provided by 360DMax management tools.

    Backed by cloud storage, your data shall always be live for you to assess it anytime, anywhere in the world. Though you might come across many tools boasting of employee productivity, yet a few cover productivity, development and automation like 360DMax.

    With the 5D approach to 360 Degrees, employee feedback is no longer a rigorous time-consuming activity but a fully automotive, cost-effective process yielding tons of comprehensive reports.

    Maintain employee reporting anonymity with the 360 Degrees Max tool, as you are no longer bound to share data with any external design agency. Create your own questionnaires and test employee competencies with your own parameters.

    Premium Administered Service

    In accordance with the plan and timeline we have established, we will manage the project’s online component on your behalf. This makes it easier to make sure, when necessary, that your 360° feedback project fits into the schedule of your wider corporate development objectives.

    These services, which can be individually negotiated, often consist of:

    • ·Placing individual 360s
    • Ensuring the entry of the predetermined mix of respondents
    • Following up on participants’ development
    • Reminding the appraisee and replies three times before referring them to the organization for action
    • Communicate with the appraisee about any failed email addresses
    • Obtaining finished reports and sending them on time
    • At predetermined intervals (often every two weeks), the client receives an overview of the project and any necessary actions.

    You can focus on your core competency with confidence knowing that 360 DMAX will ensure that your 360 process works well and that participants finish their 360 in time to fulfil your larger agenda.
