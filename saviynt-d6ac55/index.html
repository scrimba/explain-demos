Intelligent Requests

See how Saviynt boosts productivity, improves decision making, and reduces over-provisioning of access.

This video showcases how Saviynt cuts down the noise for end users and approvers with:
  • A greater understanding of what access they should have and how to get it
  • The ability to make more informed decisions
  • Flexible trust scoring that provides greater request accuracy
 

Intelligent Certifications

Learn how Saviynt not only helps reduce fatigue and improve compliance, but also helps organizations remain audit ready.

This video underscores Saviynt’s ability to reduce rubber stamping and overprovisioning by:
  • Providing greater context as to why a decision should or should not be taken
  • Understanding what influences trust scores and how scores can be adjusted
  • Automating decision making with Smart Review capabilities
